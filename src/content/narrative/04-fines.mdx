## {finesLayer.label}

<p class="section-subtitle">{finesLayer.subtitle}</p>

Compliance fines are the second invisible tax. Unlike trade deductions — which reflect commercial terms — fines penalise operational failures: mislabelled cases, damaged pallets, late trucks, spoiled product. They signal process gaps, not negotiating outcomes. Total compliance fines across the analysis period: $2.2M.

Spoilage is the dominant fine category at $1.4M — more than all other compliance categories combined. UNFI alone accounts for $446K in spoilage fines across 545 events, suggesting a cold-chain or shelf-life problem specific to the distributor's logistics network. Walmart ($426K, 340 events) and KeHE ($277K, 377 events) follow.

Label fines total $339K, with Walmart accounting for $230K of that (771 events, averaging $298 each). Costco's label fines are fewer (46 events) but more punitive at $913 per violation. Late delivery fines ($190K) are concentrated at Walmart ($115K), reflecting OTIF penalty programs.
