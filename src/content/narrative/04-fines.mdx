## {finesLayer.label}

<p class="section-subtitle">{finesLayer.subtitle}</p>

Compliance fines are the second invisible tax. Unlike trade deductions — which reflect commercial terms — fines penalise operational failures: mislabelled cases, damaged pallets, late trucks, spoiled product. They signal process gaps, not negotiating outcomes. Total compliance fines in the year to March 2026: $778K.

Spoilage is the dominant fine category at $527K — more than all other compliance categories combined. UNFI alone accounts for $167K in spoilage fines across 205 events, suggesting a cold-chain or shelf-life problem specific to the distributor's logistics network. Walmart ($160K, 124 events) and KeHE ($98K, 139 events) follow.

Label fines total $106K, with Walmart accounting for $79K of that (277 events, averaging $285 each). Late delivery fines ($68K) are concentrated at Walmart ($37K), reflecting OTIF penalty programs.
