import layers from '../../data/layers.json';

export const netLayer = layers[4];
export const totalContribution = netLayer.channels.reduce((s, c) => s + c.value, 0);
export const walmartContribution = netLayer.channels.find(c => c.channel_name === 'Walmart')?.value || 0;
export const walmartShare = ((walmartContribution / totalContribution) * 100).toFixed(0);
export const dtcContribution = netLayer.channels.find(c => c.channel_name === 'DTC')?.value || 0;
export const dtcShare = ((dtcContribution / totalContribution) * 100).toFixed(0);

## What this means for capital allocation

The numbers above point to three actions, in order of expected return.

**1. Grow retail volume.** Retail channels produced a 50.6% contribution margin — 5.3 points above distribution's 45.3%. Deduction profiles are more complex (nine types versus five), but the higher per-dollar return more than compensates. Incremental volume here delivers the highest marginal return of any channel type.

**2. Restructure retailer dispute triage.** Operational overhead from dispute resolution totals $186.0K/yr, driven almost entirely by retailers: Walmart alone accounts for $41.8K/yr on ~637 disputes per year. Automate low-value claims, raise the filing threshold, or eliminate disputes on channels where recovery is negligible. This is the largest controllable cost in the retailer waterfall after COGS.

**3. Review Costco economics.** Costco has the lowest contribution margin (46.3%) and the highest trade deduction rate (1.5% of revenue). It is the weakest retailer on margin. A trade-term renegotiation or promotional rebalancing could close part of the three-point gap between Costco and the best-performing retailers.
