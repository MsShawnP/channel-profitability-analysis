import layers from '../../data/layers.json';

export const netLayer = layers[4];
export const totalContribution = netLayer.channels.reduce((s, c) => s + c.value, 0);
export const walmartContribution = netLayer.channels.find(c => c.channel_name === 'Walmart')?.value || 0;
export const walmartShare = ((walmartContribution / totalContribution) * 100).toFixed(0);
export const dtcContribution = netLayer.channels.find(c => c.channel_name === 'DTC')?.value || 0;
export const dtcShare = ((dtcContribution / totalContribution) * 100).toFixed(0);

## What this means for capital allocation

The numbers above point to three actions, in order of expected return.

**1. Accelerate DTC growth.** DTC contributes 73 cents per revenue dollar — nearly three times what wholesale channels deliver. At $1.3M in revenue, it already contributes {dtcShare}% of total net contribution on just 5.5% of revenue. The margin advantage is structural (full retail pricing, zero deductions, zero fines), not temporary. Every additional DTC dollar is worth nearly three wholesale dollars in contribution terms.

**2. Reduce spoilage across distributors.** Spoilage fines total $527K — more than all other compliance categories combined. UNFI alone accounts for $167K across 205 events. This signals a cold-chain or shelf-life problem that warrants joint investigation with the distributor partners. Unlike label fines (fixable with a QA gate), spoilage requires operational changes to packaging, logistics timing, or product mix.

**3. Root-cause unclassified deductions.** "Unclassified" chargebacks total $1.2M across all channels. These are deductions Cinderhaven cannot explain from remittance data alone. Some portion is legitimate; some portion is recoverable. The dispute process already recovers $359K annually — a positive return on $324K in triage cost — but that recovery is concentrated at Walmart and Costco. Closing the documentation gap on the remaining $1.2M in vague deductions (particularly Walmart's $456K) is where the largest incremental recovery sits.
