# Where the money actually goes

Cinderhaven averages {new Intl.NumberFormat('en-US', { style: 'currency', currency: 'USD', maximumFractionDigits: 1, notation: 'compact' }).format(totalRevenue)}/yr in gross revenue across ten channels (FY2024–FY2026). But gross revenue is a vanity metric. Between cost of goods, trade deductions, compliance fines, and dispute-resolution labour, every channel surrenders a different fraction of each dollar before it reaches contribution margin.

Retail channels returned $53,000 more per million deployed than distribution. That variance — driven by COGS intensity and the operational overhead of retailer dispute triage — is where capital allocation decisions should start.
