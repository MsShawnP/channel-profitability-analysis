## {revenueLayer.label}

<p class="section-subtitle">{revenueLayer.subtitle}</p>

Revenue concentration is the first thing to notice. Walmart alone accounts for 44% of the total — a dependency that makes every Walmart-specific deduction a material event. The top three channels (Walmart, UNFI, KeHE) represent 73% of gross revenue.

This is not unusual for a specialty food brand at Cinderhaven's scale. Distribution economics favour consolidation: fewer ship-tos, higher fill rates, better slot efficiency. The question is whether that concentration survives once costs are allocated back.
