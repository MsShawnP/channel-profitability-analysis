## Margin evolution

<p class="section-subtitle">Five quarters ending March 2026</p>

Contribution margins were broadly stable through 2025 before compressing in the first quarter of 2026. The compression was not uniform — it hit the largest channels hardest.

Costco's margins swung across an eleven-point range: from 89% in early 2025 to nearly 95% at mid-year, then down to 83.5% by March 2026. The volatility reflects the channel's concentrated deduction pattern — a single quarter of heavy chargebacks (Q3 2025: $44K in deductions alone) can move the margin by six points.

Walmart held steady at 92% for four consecutive quarters before dropping to 89% in early 2026, driven by a surge in trade deductions. At Walmart's scale, a three-point margin move represents roughly $68K in lost contribution per quarter.

Among distributors, both UNFI and KeHE show a persistent downward drift — UNFI from 92.3% to 88.7%, KeHE from 92.7% to 88.4%. The decline is gradual and consistent across five quarters, suggesting structural pressure on distributor margins rather than seasonal variation or one-off events.

DTC margins remained structurally flat at 71.5–71.7% across all five quarters. This confirms the channel's margin gap is a permanent feature of its cost structure — not a temporary condition that might improve with scale or operational maturity.
