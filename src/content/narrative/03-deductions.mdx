## {deductionsLayer.label}

<p class="section-subtitle">{deductionsLayer.subtitle}</p>

Trade deductions are the first invisible tax after COGS. They never appear on the income statement as a line item — they arrive as post-invoice adjustments, chargebacks, and remittance shortfalls. Across all channels, Cinderhaven surrenders roughly $276K/yr to trade deductions (annual average, FY2024–FY2026).

The largest categories are pricing errors ($72K/yr), promotional billbacks ($69K/yr), and slotting fees ($50K/yr), spread fairly evenly across channels. Trade deduction rates range from 0.5% to 1.1% of revenue across wholesale channels, with Costco highest at 1.1% and Walmart close behind at 1.0%.

For the wholesale channels, trade deductions at this scale are modest — around 1% of revenue — and broadly distributed across types rather than concentrated, suggesting normal commercial friction rather than a systemic billing problem. DTC is the exception: its direct fulfilment and platform fees absorb roughly a quarter of revenue, the structural cost of selling direct rather than a retailer deduction.
