## {deductionsLayer.label}

<p class="section-subtitle">{deductionsLayer.subtitle}</p>

Trade deductions are the first invisible tax after COGS. They never appear on the income statement as a line item — they arrive as post-invoice adjustments, chargebacks, and remittance shortfalls. Across all channels, Cinderhaven surrendered $3.1M to trade deductions in the year to March 2026.

Promotional billbacks are the largest trade deduction category at $1.7M, dominated by Walmart ($837K alone). Unclassified deductions — labelled "vague" in remittance data — total $1.2M, the second-largest category and the one most likely to yield recoveries if root-caused. Whole Foods has the highest trade deduction rate at 18.1% of revenue, followed by Walmart at 15.1%.

Distributors face the same deduction categories but at different intensities. UNFI's promo billbacks ($243K) and unclassified chargebacks ($172K) together account for 99% of its trade deductions. KeHE's pattern is similar: billbacks ($181K) and vague deductions ($127K) dominate.
